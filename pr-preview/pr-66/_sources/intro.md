# EK125 DePasquale Section Slides

This is a companion site to the main [EK125 course site](https://BU-EK125.github.io/EK125/intro.html), collecting interactive slide decks built with [marimo](https://marimo.io) for Section C1, taught by Prof. Brian DePasquale. Unlike the other EK125 sites, these aren't Jupyter notebooks -- they're live, editable, runnable Python apps that execute entirely in your browser (via WebAssembly), no installation or sign-in required.

Use the sidebar to pick a class. Each deck is embedded directly in the page below -- give it a moment to load (it's downloading a full Python runtime to your browser the first time), then navigate with the arrow keys or on-screen controls.

Some decks also include a 🎯 **Quick Check** slide here and there -- a short multiple-choice question with A/B/C/D buttons. These are meant to be answered live, in the moment, during the lecture: take your best guess based on what was just covered, click your answer, and we'll go over the right one together in class. Answering is instant (no typing, no separate form to fill out) and there's nothing to submit afterward -- once class has moved past that slide, the question has served its purpose.

Every deck also has a 🚀 **Open in Colab** button in its top-right corner. The slides themselves are read-only during a lecture walkthrough, but that button opens the exact same code as a real, editable notebook in Google Colab -- there you can actually run each cell, change the code, and experiment on your own (just a free Google sign-in, no other setup). Use it whenever you want to dig into an example from lecture further than the slide view lets you.
